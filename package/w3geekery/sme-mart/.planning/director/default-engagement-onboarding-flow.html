ZeroBias Customer Onboarding — End-to-End Flow

From "Contact Us" to an unlocked platform: what happens at each step, where the Engagement + Project + Tasks are born, and how completing a task unlocks the next feature.

Director Parks · 2026-06-02 · grounded in zb/ui INVITE_MECHANISM_EXPLAINED + the live Activity registry (prod)

The one-sentence version.

A prospect contacts us → a ZeroBias employee approves them → they sign in → the backend creates their default Engagement, Project, Board and onboarding tasks → and as the customer completes each onboarding task, the platform unlocks the next piece (Catalog, Labs, connectors). Step 5 — the provisioning moment — is the part we're defining now.

The invitation lifecycle (already built)

Behind steps 2–4, the platform tracks an app.organization_invitation record with a Zoho CRM lead link (crm_record_id). Its status walks this path:

New→ Sent→ Approved→ Accepted

New = lead created · Sent = synced to Zoho · Approved = ZeroBias employee green-lights (step 3) · Accepted = prospect signs in and links their principal (step 4). When the org is created, invited_org_id is set — that's the natural trigger point for Step 5 provisioning (a PlatformAdminProvisioning event handler already exists to hang it on).

Ownership chain: Project > Board > Tasks. A Board is a child of a Project; Tasks are owned by a Board. So the open decision isn't "engagement or project owns the tasks" directly — it's which Project the onboarding Board hangs off: the Engagement node (also a platform.Project) or the governed Delivery Project. The tasks follow whichever Project owns that Board.

The customer initially sees only Projects App / Engagements / their engagement, where the onboarding tasks live. Everything else is locked until tasks are completed.

3 The Req / Sat Unlock Model

This is the heart of it. Every onboarding gate is a pair of linked tasks that straddle the line between ZeroBias and the customer:

  • Req (Requirement) — lives on the ZeroBias platform side. The assertion: "this customer must sign the EULA / MSA / pay / etc."
  • Sat (Satisfaction) — lives on the customer side, on their onboarding board. The action: "Sign the MSA."
  • When the customer completes the Sat task, it satisfies the Req, which fires a workflow that either auto-unlocks the feature or notifies an admin to unlock it (depending on the gate).
